# GasFlow ERP

GasFlow ERP - Enterprise Resource Planning system for gas management and monitoring.

## Project Objectives

- Customer-owned cylinders management
- Cylinder registration and barcode tracking
- Safety inspections and approvals
- LPG, oxygen, and other gas-filling operations
- Cash and credit sales
- Tax invoices
- Complete accounting ledger
- Multiple branches support
- Central owner-level monitoring
- English and Urdu interfaces

## Features

- Cylinder inventory management with barcode tracking
- Gas filling operations for LPG, oxygen, and other gases
- Sales management (cash and credit transactions)
- Tax invoice generation and management
- Complete accounting ledger system
- Multi-branch support with centralized monitoring
- Safety inspection tracking and approvals
- Bilingual support (English and Urdu)
- Customer cylinder ownership tracking
- Real-time dashboard and reporting

## Tech Stack

- React 19
- Vite
- TailwindCSS
- Framer Motion
- React Router
- Axios
- TanStack Query
